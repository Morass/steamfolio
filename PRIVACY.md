# Steamfolio privacy policy

Steamfolio reads game names and sales and wishlist reports from the Steamworks pages available to you while you are signed in. It uses those figures to calculate and display the portfolio view you request. The figures stay in the current browser tab and are not saved by Steamfolio.

Steamfolio saves your selected game IDs, chosen statistic, and date range in Chrome's local extension storage so they are available the next time you open the pane. It does not save your Steamworks password or cookies. Removing the extension clears its local extension storage in Chrome.

Steamfolio makes read-only requests to `partner.steampowered.com` using your existing signed-in browser session. It does not send report figures, your selections, or browsing activity to the developer or any other service. It has no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs.

The extension runs only on the Steamworks partner sites, `partner.steampowered.com` and `partner.steamgames.com`; on the second it still reads the figures from `partner.steampowered.com`. Steamworks and Chrome handle their own account and browser data under their respective policies. Steamfolio's use of data is limited to showing the portfolio statistics you request and follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
